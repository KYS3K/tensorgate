Admission control: lateness p99 under overload

8000 streams offered to a 2 vCPU server that is allowed 5000. Per-second p99 of token lateness at the client, three-minute runs, 1000 API keys.

Steady state is seconds 41 to 175; the shaded first 40 s is the ramp-up at 200 new streams per second and is not in the summary. The SLO is lateness p99 up to 150 ms (BENCHMARKS.md, 100 ms flush window), judged on the mean of the per-second p99.

Lateness is measured from the moment the request is sent, so the time a stream waited for a free slot is part of it: the 50 ms queue adds up to 50 ms to every accepted stream, the 20 ms queue up to 20 ms.

One three-minute run per line on shared vCPUs. The two default runs differ by about 2 ms in mean p99, which is the noise to compare against.
